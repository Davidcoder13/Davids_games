Geologic Time

Must-Know Concepts

  • Earth structure: crust (thin/solid), mantle (hot/plastic), outer core (liquid iron-nickel), inner core (solid iron-nickel).
  • Eras order: Precambrian → Paleozoic → Mesozoic → Cenozoic.
  • Age of Earth: ~4.54 billion years.
  • Plate tectonics: lithospheric plates move over asthenosphere, causing earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain building.
  • Evidence: continental fit, fossil matches, matching rock belts, seafloor spreading, magnetic striping, earthquake/volcano patterns.
Why plate tectonics matters It explains landform creation, natural disasters, ocean-floor age patterns, and why continents move over geologic time.
Rock Cycle

Rock Types + Cycle Words

  • Igneous: formed from cooling magma/lava (granite, basalt).
  • Sedimentary: compacted/cemented sediments or organic/chemical material (sandstone, limestone, shale).
  • Metamorphic: altered by heat/pressure without melting (marble, slate, gneiss).
Weathering: breakdown of rock in place.
Erosion: movement of weathered material by water, wind, ice, or gravity.
Magma: melted rock below surface.
Lava: magma at Earth’s surface.
Southern Ontario bedrock impact Bedrock affects building foundations, quarrying, soil fertility, groundwater movement, and aggregate resources for roads/construction.
Landform Regions of Canada

Compare Regions Quickly

Canadian ShieldAncient igneous/metamorphic rock, thin soils, many lakes from glaciation, forestry/mining/hydro activity.
Great Lakes–St. Lawrence LowlandsSedimentary rock + fertile soils, rolling plains, heavy farming/urbanization/industry.
Interior PlainsSedimentary basin, flat to rolling, agriculture and oil/gas.
CordilleraYoung fold mountains, mixed rocks, forestry/mining/tourism; glaciation carved valleys.

Glaciation effects: striations, U-shaped valleys, moraines, drumlins, outwash plains, and many lakes.

Climate Regions

Climate Controls, Maritime vs Continental, and Precipitation

6 climate factors (LOWERN): Latitude, Ocean currents, Winds/air masses, Elevation, Relief, Near water.

FactorHow it affects climate
LatitudeCloser to equator = warmer due to stronger solar energy.
Near waterWater heats/cools slowly, moderates temperature, often increases precipitation.
Ocean currentsWarm currents raise temps; cold currents cool coasts and can reduce evaporation.
Winds & air massesMove temperature/moisture characteristics from source regions.
ElevationHigher altitude = cooler temperatures.
ReliefMountains force uplift (orographic rain) and create rain shadows.
  • Vancouver (top 3): near water, prevailing westerlies, relief (Coast Mountains).
  • Toronto (top 3): latitude, near water (Great Lakes), air masses/winds.
  • Continental climate: larger annual temperature range + lower precipitation.
  • Maritime climate: smaller annual temperature range + higher precipitation.
  • Precipitation types: convectional (summer heating), orographic (mountain uplift), cyclonic/frontal (air masses meet).
Soil + Vegetation

Core Review

  • 4 soil components: minerals, organic matter, air, water.
  • Soil profile: O, A, B, C horizons (organic to parent material).
  • Permafrost: permanently frozen ground (common in northern Canada).
  • Active layer: top layer above permafrost that thaws in warm season.
  • Humus: decomposed organic material.
  • Leaching: dissolved minerals carried downward by water.
  • Loam: balanced mix of sand, silt, clay + organic matter.
  • Transition vegetation zone: Boreal & mixed forest boundary zones are transitional where species overlap.
Coniferous vs deciduous (3 differences) Needle leaves vs broad leaves, cones vs flowers/fruits, usually evergreen vs seasonal leaf drop.
Treeline + causes Treeline is where trees stop growing due to cold temps, short growing season, and poor/moist/permafrost soils (arctic and alpine areas).
Natural Disasters

Emergency Response Kit

  • Water (2 litres/person/day for at least 72 hours)
  • Non-perishable food + manual can opener
  • Flashlight + batteries, radio, first-aid kit
  • Medication, hygiene items, masks, whistle
  • Blankets/clothing, copies of key documents, cash
  • Phone charger/power bank, local contact plan
